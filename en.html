Who we are

GalaxPlus —
building since 2014

GalaxPlus brings together engineers, architects and builders who have spent more than a decade setting the turnkey standard in Kyiv: from the first sketch to a fully fitted-out space. We work on housing, offices, retail sites and industrial facilities — and everywhere we follow one principle: complex decisions should be clear to the client.

Every project is led by a dedicated manager, and technical supervision is carried out by the specialists who designed and installed those utilities themselves. That is why we spot risks before they arise and deliver a result that matches the design down to the last millimetre.
